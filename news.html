Athletics &amp; Team Spirit

Annual Inter-House Sports Gala 2026

A thrilling day of track and field heroics, camaraderie, and spirited competition across all four school houses.

From heart-pounding 100m sprints and mixed relay battles to long jump and tug-of-war, our student athletes showcased remarkable resilience, sportsmanship, and discipline. Parents, staff, and alumni cheered on as Phoenix House took the coveted Overall Championship Cup, while Eagle House set two new school track records.

  • Champion House: Phoenix House with 482 total points
  • Track Milestone: New Senior 400m record clocked at 50.42s
  • Community Spirit: Over 600 parents and supporters joined the festivities

Field Trips &amp; Educational Expeditions

Extending education beyond the classroom walls through hands-on exploration and cultural discovery.

Our recent educational expeditions gave students unmatched real-world exposure:

National Science &amp; Innovation Center Trip

Junior &amp; Senior STEM scholars engaged in live renewable energy demonstrations, robotics workshops, and planetarium astronomy sessions.

Ecological Reserve &amp; Wildlife Conservation Tour

Geography and Biology clubs carried out botanical sampling, water biodiversity assessments, and habitat conservation tree-planting.

Historical Heritage &amp; Civic Leadership Tour

Arts and humanities classes toured regional museum archives, legislative chambers, and artisan craft co-operatives.
